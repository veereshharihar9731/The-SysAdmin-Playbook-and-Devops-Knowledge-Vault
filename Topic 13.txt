Reverse proxy
A reverse proxy is a server that receives requests from clients and forwards them to one or more backend servers on the clients' behalf.

Load balancer
A load balancer distributes incoming traffic across multiple backend servers according to a configured policy.
* Round Robin -->Requests are assigned to servers in rotation.
* Weighted Routing --> Servers with higher configured weights receive a larger share of traffic.
* Least Connection --> New connections are directed toward the server with fewer active connections, according to the implementation's rules.
* Health Based Routing --> Servers that fail configured health checks can be removed from normal traffic until they recover.
