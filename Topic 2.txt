USERS AND GROUPS

User: An individual employee with their own login account.
Group: A team of employees who need similar access to files or applications.
Permissions: Rules that determine what each user or group can do.

File			Purpose
/etc/passwd		User account information
/etc/shadow		Password hashes and password-aging information; restricted access
/etc/group		Group information and supplementary memberships
/etc/gshadow	Protected group administration information
